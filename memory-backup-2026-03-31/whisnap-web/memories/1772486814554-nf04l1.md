DECISION: Remove 'AI' from local/on-device transcription contexts across all SEO pages.

REPLACEMENT PATTERNS:
- 'local AI models' → 'local models'
- 'on-device AI' → 'on-device transcription'
- 'AI models running on-device' → 'on-device transcription'
- 'AI transcribes your voice' → 'Whisnap transcribes your voice'
- 'AI Transcription App' → 'Speech-to-Text App'
- 'Whisper AI models' → 'Whisper models'

KEEP 'AI' for:
- Cloud AI transcription (actual service description)
- Context-aware AI formatting (Cloud Mode feature)
- Cloud AI providers

WHISPER RULE:
- Max one mention per page as an example: 'models like Whisper' or 'including OpenAI Whisper'
- Sections should say 'Local Models' not 'Whisper Models'
- Reason: we're selling Whisnap, not Whisper's brand

REASONING: 'AI' is overused/meaningless for speech recognition models. Users understand 'local transcription' better. Whisper is a third-party model — leading with it sells OpenAI's brand, not ours.
