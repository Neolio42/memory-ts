PHILOSOPHY:
 PRINCIPLE: The deck is structured as a liberation narrative, not a feature comparison. Each section unlocks the next.
 SOURCE: Multiple rounds of user feedback demanding story over features ('no real story, no differentiator')
 APPLICATION:
  ACT 1 (Slides 1-5): Problem — show the mess of human voice, then the industry forcing you to pay with money or time
  ACT 2 (Slides 6-10): Liberation — Whisnap is free, local, uncapped. One card per feature building anticipation.
  ACT 3 (Slides 11-15): Intelligence — not just transcription, it thinks. Shows output adapting to Slack/Gmail/Git.
  ACT 4 (Slide 16): Credibility — look at the engineering behind this
  ACT 5 (Slides 17-18): Validation — market exists and is growing without marketing
  ACT 6 (Slides 19-20): Founder + Vision — 'I pushed it to production. This is what I'm building.'
