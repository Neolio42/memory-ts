WORKFLOW:
PATTERN: For Whisnap pitch deck slides, visual elements + maximum one key line of text on the slide. All explanatory content goes in speaker notes.

WHEN: Every time editing or creating a slide for this deck

WHY: User explicitly said 'too much text on slides 4 and 5 — a lot of information that should be spoken not written down.' Repeated this twice. The speaker is supposed to deliver context verbally. Slides are visual anchors, not teleprompters.

SPECIFIC EXAMPLES:
- FREE TIER: Just 'FREE TIER' label, app mock animation, 'On-device. Private. Free.' — nothing else
- CLOUD TIER: Just 'CLOUD TIER' label, before/after transformation visual, '$10/month' — nothing else
- Do NOT add bullet lists of features, explanatory paragraphs, or sub-headlines that duplicate what would be spoken

EXCEPTION: Team slide has multi-line founder story text (intentional — this IS the slide content, delivered as text reveals)
