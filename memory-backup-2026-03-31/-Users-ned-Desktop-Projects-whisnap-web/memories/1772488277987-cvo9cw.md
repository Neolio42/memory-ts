PRINCIPLE: Whisnap homepage tone is the canonical reference — 'We don't compete on features. We compete on business model.'
SOURCE: components/HeroWhisnap.tsx, components/HonestComparison.tsx — the homepage was written by the product owner with intentional voice
APPLICATION:
- Hero: 1 short paragraph max, no filler
- Claims: only verifiable facts — no 'Join thousands', no 'studies show', no percentages without source
- Competitor comparisons: include honest 'Where X Wins' section — credibility through fairness
- Feature cards: 1-2 sentences each, every sentence must say something useful
- Meeting notes specifically: Whisnap is NOT a recorder, it voice-types at cursor (during meetings) OR batch-transcribes recordings (after meetings)
- Apple Dictation: does work offline AND system-wide — acknowledge it, differentiate on batch upload + AI formatting
- Google Voice Typing: works great but only in Google Docs — that's the entire differentiator
