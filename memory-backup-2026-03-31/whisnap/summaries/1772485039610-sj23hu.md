Audited a broken hotkey/keybind system where swapping push-to-talk (bare FN) and toggle recording (FN+Space) silently breaks both. Root cause: the FN coordinator in Rust is hardcoded — bare FN always routes to push-to-talk handler, FN+key always routes to toggle handler — making the 'configurable' keybinds effectively non-swappable. Also discovered that FN+key combos don't need the 300ms language-switch hold threshold that bare FN does.
