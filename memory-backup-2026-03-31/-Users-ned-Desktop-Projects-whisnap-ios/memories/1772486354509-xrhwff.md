INSIGHT: The repeated 'WARNING: Did not receive handshake message from the host after waiting ~2 seconds' and multiple viewDidLoad calls WITHOUT viewWillAppear are NORMAL iOS behavior.
BEFORE: Treated as errors to fix, led to misdiagnosis.
AFTER: These are spurious XPC probe launches — iOS starts the extension process multiple times to probe endpoints. Only the final 'real' launch reaches viewWillAppear.
APPLICATION:
- Do NOT do heavy work in viewDidLoad (it runs even on throwaway launches)
- CFPrefs 'kCFPreferencesAnyUser with Container: (null)' warning = App Group UserDefaults not yet available on spurious launches — still works on real launch
- '[S:2-Xr] Error received: Invalidated by remote connection' = normal XPC teardown from throwaway processes
- REAL problems to look for: actual crash logs, watchdog kills (process exceeds time/memory), not XPC warnings
