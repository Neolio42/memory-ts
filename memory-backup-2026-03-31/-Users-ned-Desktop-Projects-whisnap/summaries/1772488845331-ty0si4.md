Full audit and research session for integrating Parakeet TDT models into Whisnap (macOS Tauri speech-to-text app). Evaluated all viable Rust options (sherpa-rs, parakeet-rs, ort, transcribe-rs) and concluded sherpa-rs v0.6.8 stable is the correct path. CoreML was definitively ruled out — it's slower than CPU for transducer models across all ONNX approaches on Apple Silicon.
